*The "marketmood_v1" formula* (`62_calculate_market_mood.py`), built entirely on `prices_daily` (deliberately not mixing in `live_prices`, so the exact same code scores a live "today" or backtests any past date with zero special-casing):
1. **Breadth** — % of the Nifty 500 whose close rose vs. the previous trading day.
2. **Momentum** — % of the Nifty 500 trading above their own trailing 50-day simple moving average.
3. **Volatility** — each stock's most recent 20-day realized volatility (stdev of daily returns) ranked as a percentile against its OWN trailing ~1-year history of 20-day volatility readings; the market-wide median percentile is inverted (`(1 - median) * 100`) so a calmer-than-usual market scores higher.
`overall_score` = plain equal-weighted average of the three — v1, documented as such (same as TrueScore's own v1 start). A short auto-written commentary sentence (e.g. *"Markets mixed today — breadth weak, momentum soft, calmer than usual."*) is built from banded thresholds on the actual numbers, never AI-generated.

`61_add_market_mood_table.sql` creates `market_mood` (one row per `run_date` + `formula_version`, so a future v2 can be backtested without overwriting v1's history), with the standard RLS + anon-SELECT public-read pattern every frontend-facing table needs.

**Validated before shipping**, per Avdhoot's own chosen approach: ran `python 62_calculate_market_mood.py --backtest 90` against 90 real trading days BEFORE touching the frontend. ## Session 11, part 13 — root cause found and fixed: SWIGGY/FIRSTCRY/THERMAX shareholding failures

Avdhoot asked specifically why these 3 (of the ~6-7 recurring failures in `25_shareholding_refresh.py`) were failing. Rather than guess, built and ran two rounds of one-off diagnostic scripts (`26_diagnose_shareholding_failures.py`, not part of the regular pipeline) against real NSE filings for these tickers:

- Round 1 confirmed the filings download fine (HTTP 200, real XBRL content) — so it wasn't a network/fetch problem.
- Round 2 dumped every `(contextRef, value)` pair found in one real filing per ticker. Found `EmployeeBenefitsTrusts_ContextI` present in all 3, with values (5.08% SWIGGY, 6.98% FIRSTCRY, 5.46% THERMAX) matching the `employeeTrusts` field already visible in NSE's raw filing-list JSON.

**Root cause:** NSE's shareholding XBRL schema was revised (this schema version is dated "2025-10", newer than whichever version `25_shareholding_refresh.py` was originally built/tested against) to report Employee Benefit Trust holdings as their own top-level category — separate from, and NOT included inside, `PublicShareholding_ContextI`'s total. The sanity check was doing exactly its job — just against a data shape it hadn't been built to expect.

**Fix:** `25_shareholding_refresh.py` now also extracts `EmployeeBenefitsTrusts_ContextI` and folds it into `public_pct` (the honest bucket for "not promoter-controlled, not a tracked institution") rather than adding a 5th DB column/chart segment for what's usually a small, non-controlling holding. Pushed to Avdhoot's computer and verified landed.

## Session 11, part 2 — TrueScore distress guardrail added (fixes the Session 10 part 9 sanity-check failure)

Deliberately simple/conservative (not a new ML model), and the raw component scores (`relative_valuation_score`, `ml_rank_score`) are left untouched in the database for transparency/audit — only the combined score and rating are overridden.

## Session 9 — sector-neutral robustness check

## Session 6 — Step 6 correction (IMPORTANT, read before touching TrueScore)
